Lead name cleaner

"Hi DR MATTHEW, saw that ACME HOLDINGS, LLC is hiring" gives away a mail merge in the first line. Upload your list and get it back with a clean company name, a clean first name, and a flag on every row a person should read before it sends.

What it does

Company names

  • Strips Inc, LLC, Ltd, Corp, Company, GmbH, Pty Ltd and the rest
  • Cuts taglines after | or a dash, and city-and-state tails
  • Takes the trading name after "dba"
  • Drops (parentheses), ™ and ®, and a second-language duplicate
  • Fixes ALL CAPS and all lowercase, keeps HubSpot and IBM as they are
  • Leaves "Med Spa" and "Valley AG" alone
  • Returns blank for N/A, Self-employed, Retired, Confidential

First names

  • Removes Dr, Mr, Ms, MD, PhD, Jr, emoji and stars
  • Uses the nickname: Kathryn (Katie) becomes Katie
  • Cuts a full name or a hiring notice down to the first name
  • Fixes PAUL and javonne, keeps DeAndrea and O'Brien
  • Flags Admin, Info, Sales, single initials, and a name that's really the company
  • Keeps names in other scripts as written, flagged for review

needs_review = yes means a person should look before the row sends. A blank is never filled with "there" or "your team": a row with no usable name usually isn't a real person, and a generic greeting just sends a worse email.

Everything runs in your browser. Your file is never uploaded anywhere.
